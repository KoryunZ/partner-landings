FAQ

Common questions

Can foreigners buy property in Bali?

Yes, usually through a long-term leasehold. The projects we offer come with leases of about 28–35 years, many with options to extend. The notary checks the land title before you pay the main amount.

Can foreigners buy property in Phuket?

Yes. Foreigners can own condominium units in their own name within the building's foreign-ownership quota. Villas are usually bought through a leasehold or a company structure. We explain the right option for each project.

Are the rental yields guaranteed?

No. The yields shown are developer projections based on occupancy and nightly rates. Real income depends on the market and how the property is managed. We'll show you the pessimistic scenario too.

Are there payment plans?

Many off-plan projects allow installments during construction, for example 30% upfront and the rest over 16–24 months. Ready properties are usually paid in full after due diligence.

Do I have to travel to buy?

No. Viewings can be done by video and contracts signed remotely. If you want to see the property in person, we can organize the visit.

What does your service cost?

The consultation and selection are free for buyers. You pay the property price plus the developer's standard costs, such as notary fees and taxes where they apply.
