FAQ

Common questions

Is citizenship by investment legal?

Yes. These are official government programs set out in each country's law. Applications go through licensed agents and strict due diligence checks.

Do I have to give up my current citizenship?

The countries listed allow dual citizenship. Whether you can keep your current citizenship depends on your home country's law, which we check during the assessment.

Do I need to live in the country?

For most citizenship programs, no. Antigua asks for 5 days within the first 5 years, Argentina for one visit for biometrics, and Portugal HSV for about 7 days a year.

Can I include my family?

Yes. Spouses and children can be included in all programs, and many also accept parents. Age limits differ by country and are shown in the comparison table.

What is the total cost?

The minimum investment or donation is the biggest part. On top come government, due diligence and processing fees, which depend on the family size. You get an exact breakdown after the assessment.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Every application is reviewed by the government. We check your profile in advance to avoid applying where the risk of refusal is high.
